League Rules

GIFFL Revisited Constitution

Number of Teams

The league consists of four teams.

Rosters

  • 2 Quarterbacks (QB)
  • 3 Running Backs (RB)
  • 3 Wide Receivers (WR)
  • 2 Tight Ends (TE)
  • 2 Placekickers (PK)

Starting Lineup

  • 1 QB
  • 2 RB
  • 2 WR
  • 1 TE
  • 1 PK

Scoring

Scoring is the same for all players:

  • Touchdown scored by any method: 6 points
  • Touchdown thrown: 4 points
  • Field Goal kicked: 3 points
  • Extra Point kicked: 1 point

Standings

Standings are based solely on total points scored.

No head‑to‑head matchups are used.

  • Top two teams advance to the Superb Bowl
  • Bottom two teams meet in the Superfluous Bowl

Transactions

  • Each team receives 5 transactions for the season.
  • The two Superb Bowl teams each receive 1 bonus transaction before the championship.

Schedule

  • 15 regular season weeks
  • Week 16 is Championship Week
